About Me

My name is Chengzhi ("chen-jeh") * Zhang 章程之. I am originally from the picturesque city of Hangzhou in China, which proudly boasts three UNESCO World Heritage sites. But, due to a random walk, I am now a Ph.D. student at Georgia Tech advised by Professor Brian Magerko at the Expressive Machinery Lab. Before that, I received my M.S. in Computational Design from Carnegie Mellon University and my Bachelor's in Industrial Design from Hunan University, China.
In my free time, I love to study/work at local ☕️ cafes, listen to 🎧 podcasts (one of my favorites: 8fenban ), learn new languages (now learning 📚Japanese), and play with 🐱 my cat.

My research interests are:
  • Designing and developing interactive experiences that enhance public understanding of AI in informal learning environments, with a focus on generative AI systems like ChatGPT.
  • Investigating novel embodied and tangible ways of interacting and improvising with AI.
  • Exploring methods to better leverage AI for enhancing user experience, especially for professionals like UX designers and artists.
